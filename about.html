About Pro Master

Experienced hands for walls, surfaces and renovation details.

We help clients improve their properties with honest communication, careful preparation and clean workmanship.

Our approach

We treat every surface as part of the final result.

Pro Master Wallcovering was built around practical craftsmanship. We understand that a beautiful finish depends on what happens before it: removal, preparation, alignment, installation and cleanup.

Our work covers wallpaper fitting, painting, demolition and renovation, drywall installation, tile work and flooring. Whether the project is a single feature wall or a broader renovation, the goal is the same: deliver a result the client can trust.

What clients can expect

Professional standards from first conversation to final cleanup.

Careful preparation

We protect the workspace and prepare walls, floors and surfaces before installation begins.

Quality materials

We use reliable materials and proven methods for durable, professional-looking results.

Clear communication

We keep scope, timing and expectations clear so clients know what is happening at each stage.
